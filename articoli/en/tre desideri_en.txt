titolo: Three Wishes
pubblicazione: 2026-06-14
testo:

I'm having a coffee in a bakery that also serves specialty coffee. The barista is euphoric about having me try the 2025 world champion coffee. He literally shouts "on se régale."
A video about the NBA finals, an interview with a spry 70-year-old who says he came from LA and shouts "I paid 55 thousand dollars for the ticket, it's a magnificent thing, I'm in seventh heaven."

The first one reminds me of the second, and then I make the connection that I've seen the opening scene of Mad Max (the first one) and their mood is exactly this. A madman driving a car at 100 km/h saying how much he loves escaping from the police.
